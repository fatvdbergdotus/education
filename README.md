# Education Portfolio

This portfolio presents my educational philosophy, teaching experience, and professional development as an educator. It brings together my academic background, practical teaching experience, and reflections on educational methods and didactic approaches. Having worked in both academic research and higher education, I believe that effective teaching requires more than subject-matter expertise: it also requires the ability to understand students' learning needs, design meaningful educational activities, provide constructive feedback, and continuously reflect on one's own teaching practice.

The portfolio is structured around three learning outcomes: Guiding Learning Processes (LO1), Designing Education (LO2), and Professional Development as a Teacher (LO3). Together, these learning outcomes provide a framework for examining how I support students throughout their learning journey, develop and assess educational activities, and continuously improve my professional competencies. They also reflect my belief that education is a shared and ongoing process in which both students and teachers learn, explore, make mistakes, and develop. This portfolio is a work in progress and will continue to evolve as I gain further teaching experience, receive feedback, and deepen my understanding of educational theory and practice.

## Theory
- [Education Methods](https://github.com/fatvdbergdotus/education/blob/main/methods/README.md):
Education methods describe different approaches to organising and facilitating the learning process, varying in terms of student autonomy, responsibility, flexibility, and structure. **Course-based** learning provides a structured curriculum with predefined learning objectives and activities, while **demand-based** learning focuses on individual learning needs and knowledge gaps. **Research-based** learning emphasises scientific investigation, experimentation, and critical analysis to develop independent problem-solving and research skills. **Open learning** offers flexibility and autonomy, allowing students to determine what, when, where, and how they learn. The suitability of each method depends on the educational objectives, students' prior knowledge, and the desired balance between instructor guidance and student independence.

## Learning Outcomes
- [LO1 Guiding Learning Processes](https://github.com/fatvdbergdotus/education/blob/main/LO1/README.md):
This learning outcome focuses on the ability to **guide and support students** throughout their learning process. It involves understanding and applying different educational approaches, including course-based, demand-based, research-based, and open learning. It also covers selecting appropriate assessment methods and didactic approaches, as well as supervising students to support their academic progress, personal development, and achievement of learning objectives.

Frameworks used: [Self-Regulated Learning – Zimmerman(https://github.com/fatvdbergdotus/education/blob/main/LO1/README.md#framework_zimmerman) [Zone of Proximal Development – Vygotsky](https://github.com/fatvdbergdotus/education/blob/main/LO1/README.md#framework_vygotsky)

- [LO2 Designing Education](https://github.com/fatvdbergdotus/education/blob/main/LO2/README.md):
This learning outcome focuses on **designing effective educational activities**, courses, and assessment strategies. It involves defining appropriate learning outcomes, applying Bloom's taxonomy to establish the required levels of cognitive development, and designing assessment processes that align with educational objectives. It also addresses different assessment types (diagnostic, formative, and summative) and the importance of validity, reliability, and transparency in assessment.

- [LO3 Professional Development as a Teacher](https://github.com/fatvdbergdotus/education/blob/main/LO3/README.md):
This learning outcome focuses on **continuous professional development** and improving one's effectiveness as an educator. It involves reflecting on teaching practices, identifying personal strengths and areas for improvement, developing pedagogical and didactic competencies, and incorporating feedback and new insights into teaching activities. The objective is to become a reflective and continuously developing professional who adapts teaching practices to students' needs and changing educational requirements.
